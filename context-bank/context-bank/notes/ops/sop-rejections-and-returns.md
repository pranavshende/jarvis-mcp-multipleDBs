# SOP — Rejections, appeals & returns

- Rejected units are held 14 days. Member can pay return shipping ($12/box) or authorise disposal.
- Appeals go through a Discord ticket (`rejected_items`). Inspection specialist reviews photos within 3 business days.
- If an appeal is upheld, we pay at the original pre-quote price.
- Three upheld resealed rejections from the same person in 90 days → account review by Hannah. "Same person" is a
  judgement call: we don't have a reliable way to link accounts (see `members/duplicate-accounts.md`).
