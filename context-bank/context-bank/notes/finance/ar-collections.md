# AR & collections

_Owner: Kenji Watanabe_

- Standard terms are **net-30** from invoice date. Some live sellers are on net-15. Brand-new partners prepay for
  their first three orders.
- Day +1 past due: automated reminder from the portal.
- Day +7: account manager emails.
- Day +15: account manager calls, Kenji copied. New orders require prepay.
- Day +30: Sam is told. Credit hold.
- Day +60: collections agency or legal.

The portal does **not** enforce credit holds automatically. Account managers are supposed to check AR before
confirming a quote. In practice, big accounts get waved through.
