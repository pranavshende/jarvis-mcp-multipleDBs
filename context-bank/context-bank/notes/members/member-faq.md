# Member FAQ (pinned in Discord #faq)

**How long until I get paid?** Inspection is usually 1–2 days after delivery. Then PayPal same day, ACH 2–3
business days, wallet instant.

**Why was my box rejected?** Our inspectors photograph every reject. Open a `rejected_items` ticket to appeal.

**Can I use two accounts?** No. One account per person. (We can't always tell, but please don't.)

**My package says delivered but not received?** Give us 2 business days, then open a `package_status` ticket.

**Can I cash out my wallet?** Yes — open a `cashout` ticket. Minimum $25.
