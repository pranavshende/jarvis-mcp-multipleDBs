# Glossary

_Maintained by Priya. If a word means two things, write both down here._

- **Pre-quote** — the fixed price a member sees on the buy list when they create a submission. Locked for 7 days.
- **Buy list** — the list of products we are currently buying and at what price. Updated Mondays by Pricing.
- **Submission (SUB-…)** — a member's sell order. One submission can arrive as one or more boxes.
- **Package** — a physical box that arrives at the dock. Has a carrier tracking number. One package usually holds
  several products and many units. Ops sometimes says "package" when they mean "submission". Finance sometimes
  says "package" when they mean "unit". Sorry.
- **Unit** — one sellable item (one booster box, one hobby case, one tube of coins).
- **Received** — scanned at the dock. Not the same as checked in.
- **Checked in** — inspected, passed units added to stock.
- **Rejected** — every unit in the package failed inspection.
- **Pass rate / fail rate** — passed units ÷ inspected units.
- **Partner** — a business customer that buys from us through the portal.
- **Member** — an individual seller. (Some members are also partners. We don't track that anywhere.)
- **Avg cost** — see `ops/sop-average-cost.md` (ops) and `finance/cost-definition-fifo.md` (finance). They are
  not the same number.
- **Listed stock** — what partners see as available in the portal. Fed from the WMS by the stock-sync job.
- **On hand** — physical units in the WMS, including units reserved for open orders.
- **Wallet** — member store credit. Liability on our books until cashed out.
- **Index** — MSC Sealed Index on the market page. 100 = 22 July 2026.
