# New hire onboarding checklist

- [ ] Google Workspace account (Sam or Mei)
- [ ] Slack: #general, #ops, your team channel, #jarvis
- [ ] AI seat (see `company/how-we-use-ai.md`)
- [ ] Read: About MSC, Glossary, your team SOPs, **MSC Now** (it's long — skim the top section)
- [ ] Warehouse staff: forklift cert, scanner login (`wms_users`), safety walk with Marcus or Dana
- [ ] Engineers: GitHub org invite, read-only DB role (`msc_readonly`), `.env` from 1Password vault "eng-shared"
- [ ] Support: Discord mod role, seller admin `agent` role, read the support macros
- [ ] Finance: QuickBooks user, Gusto viewer (Kenji approves)
- [ ] Contractors: sign the contractor agreement + NDA (see `hr/contractor-guidelines.md`). Contractors do not get
      Gusto or payroll folder access.
