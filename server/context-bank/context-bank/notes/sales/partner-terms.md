# Partner terms

_Owner: Sofia Lindqvist · reviewed by Kenji · 2026-05-20_

## Payment terms
- **Net-30** from invoice date is standard for all approved partners.
- **Net-15** for live sellers and anyone with a late payment in the last 6 months.
- **Prepay** for new partners (first three orders) and anyone on credit hold.
- Longer terms (net-45/60) are **not offered**. Exceptions need written approval from Kenji **and** Sam, recorded in
  the partner's account note. There are currently **no** active exceptions.

## Pricing
- Quotes are valid 72 hours.
- Gold tier (>$500k trailing 12 months) gets ~3% off list; Silver ~1.5%.

## Shipping
- Partner pays freight on orders under $2,500. Free freight above.

## Returns
- Damaged-in-transit claims within 5 days with photos.
- No returns on sealed product for price changes.
