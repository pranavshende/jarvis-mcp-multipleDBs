# Buy list policy (member-facing summary)

- Prices are pre-quotes: lock in the price when you create a submission; ship within 7 days.
- Sealed only. We inspect every unit. Resealed, opened, damaged or wrong items are rejected.
- Payout when inspection is complete: PayPal (same day), wallet (instant), ACH (2–3 business days).
- Prices change every Monday. We may drop prices the same day if a reprint is announced.
